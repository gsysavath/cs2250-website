Song Recommender

What it Does This Kotlin app pulls from an SQLite database of 200k+ songs and asks you to rate whether you like them or not. Depending on how you answer the program will try to recommend other songs that you may like from the database based on parameters such as genre, era, etc...
Why Make This A good friend and I decided to make this application after watching a machine learning randomly one day in class. Though as high school students, we thought the actual ML part of this project would be too much, so we decided to try and derive a dumbed down statistical formula from what we learned through the videos.
